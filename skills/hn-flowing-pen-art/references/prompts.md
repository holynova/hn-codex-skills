# 可迁移提示词示例

以下为新的应用方向，不是已生成图片。每条都搭配 SKILL.md 的风格核心及至少一张真实例图输入，展示换主体、视角与主走势。

## 人物：风中的提琴手

参考 02 的面部疏密、01 的衣褶。

> Portrait frame, waist-up three-quarter view of an adult violinist on a quiet balcony. The bow creates a strong diagonal; wind lifts the coat hem toward the opposite corner. Short curved hatching models cheek and hands, directional bundles describe fabric folds, and long broken arcs suggest wind. Keep the face and space above the bow lightly hatched. Make the violin and both hands anatomically readable. No moths, lantern or lighthouse.

## 动物：雨前的鹤

参考 03 的轮廓分离、05 的留白。

> Wide horizontal composition of two cranes among reeds, one upright and one bending toward shallow water. Place their pale bodies against a denser reed bank; use feather-following fine strokes instead of flat silhouettes. Water lines stay mostly horizontal while wind bends reeds into a low arc. Leave a large quiet paper sky. No forest moon or stag.

## 建筑：雨后的拱廊

参考 04 的分面排线、01 的纵深。

> Ground-level wide view through a series of old stone arches, with a tiny distant cyclist at the vanishing point. Long perspective lines on wet paving guide the eye inward. Separate stone planes with distinct short hatch directions; render reflections with sparse broken lines, not gray washes. One side stays darker, the far opening mostly untouched paper. No spiral staircase.

## 静物：茶杯与山

参考 05 的静物展开、03 的层次。

> Square still life from a slightly elevated angle. A ceramic tea bowl and folded cloth occupy the foreground; thin steam lines gradually become distant mountain contours in the upper half. Curved sparse hatching describes the bowl, angular bundles the cloth, broken contour lines the mountains. Keep the upper quarter calm and open. Make the steam-to-mountain transition legible, with no dense vortex.

## 无人物风景：峡谷中的树

参考 01 的岩面、03 的枝干。

> Portrait view looking upward from a narrow canyon toward one wind-bent pine. Angular short lines describe fractured cliff planes; curved needles and branches trace one upward sweep. The sky is a narrow ribbon of untouched warm paper. Concentrate dark hatching near lower corners and reduce density toward the treetop.

## 针对偏差的修正

只选择与实际问题相符的一条：

- 太像版画色块：Replace broad filled shadows with separated fine pen strokes; keep visible paper gaps.
- 太像条纹滤镜：Give each material its own hatch direction and interrupt environmental curves at object boundaries.
- 画面拥挤：Reduce background line density around the focal subject and restore a larger quiet paper region.
- 纸太脏：Reduce mottling and stains; keep subtle fiber texture behind crisp ink strokes.
- 系列雷同：Change viewpoint, subject scale and dominant line movement while preserving paper and ink.
