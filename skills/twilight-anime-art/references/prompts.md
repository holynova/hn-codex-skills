# 提示词结构与示例

先完成SKILL.md要求的五图视觉校准。本文件不能替代例图。

## 共享核心

```text
Create one complete illustration, aspect ratio according to the user's request.
The input images are references for visual style, atmosphere, lighting and
rendering ONLY. Do not copy their subjects, objects or composition.
Refined hand-painted anime background illustration, clean fine linework,
simplified accurate forms, delicate painterly textures. Coral-pink / peach
light against deep indigo-violet shadows, localized amber practical light,
cyan accents where warranted. Believable light spill and material-specific
reflections. Layered depth through scale, occlusion and cooler distant planes.
Calm, warm, slightly nostalgic atmosphere. No photographic or glossy 3D
rendering, indiscriminate neon glow, extra writing or watermarks unless requested.
```

在核心后加入：用户主体与自然动作、环境/建筑/材质、镜头/焦点/前中后景、天气与具体光源、必须保留或排除的内容。按用户要求改写配色、时间、比例。青蓝可来自衣物、瓷器或环境，不要求水体；没有建筑时强调其他形体和材质；人物题材重点写自然姿态、衣物纹理与环境照明。

## 五种场景实例

把共享核心与其中一个场景段组合，再传入适合的风格例图。以下场景对应随附图像，展示写法，不是每次必画清单。

### 街角咖啡馆

```text
3:4 portrait. Eye-level view down a quiet sloping city street. A brick corner
cafe with emerald awning, amber-lit windows, bicycle and flower boxes. A woman
in a cream cardigan reads outside, a cat rests nearby, a tram approaches in
the distance. Warm light on reader and table, textured paving stones, coral
clouds over indigo city shadows. Layered street depth, intimate daily narrative.
```

### 海边列车

```text
3:4 portrait. A cream-and-teal train curves along a rocky coast toward a small
platform. A traveler with a canvas bag waits at right; wildflowers in foreground,
cyan sea and violet headlands beyond. Coral-peach twilight, amber train windows,
painted waves and deep landscape layers. Expansive and quietly anticipatory.
```

### 雨夜书店

```text
3:4 portrait, viewed from inside a bookshop. Wooden bookshelves, stacked books
and a sleeping ginger cat in foreground; amber reading lamps, rain-streaked
window. Outside, a navy-coated person with yellow umbrella crosses wet violet
paving with rosy reflections. Delicate paper and wood textures, warm interior
versus cool city dusk, layered window framing and cozy stillness.
```

### 山间旅舍

```text
3:4 portrait. Timber-and-stone lodge among fir trees below violet snow mountains.
Wooden footbridge and cyan stream lead toward amber porch where two hikers
rest beside backpacks. Coral twilight clouds, distinct snow, rock, wood and
water textures. Small people in a broad tranquil landscape, credible light
sources and atmospheric depth; no city skyline required.
```

### 屋顶晚风

```text
3:4 portrait. Young adult in loose white shirt sits on low rooftop parapet near
potted flowers and ginger cat, seen from behind at three-quarter angle. Laundry,
old radio and small telescope frame inhabited terrace. Layered low-rise homes
with amber windows recede into indigo city under peach-pink dusk. Natural
posture, painted fabric and foliage, warm light within cool shadows, reflective mood.
```

## 迁移到新题材

蓝调时刻的厨房做饭、雾中渡轮甲板、山村小巷送信、夜间陶艺工作室、秋日公园乐手，都可用同一风格。分别改变主体动作、空间结构、主要材质和镜头，不只给例图人物换衣服或替换背景城市。
